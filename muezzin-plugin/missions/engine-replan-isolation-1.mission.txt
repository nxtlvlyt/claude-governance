Niyyah: a single flaky step — a transient empty emission, one missing source — must not destroy a mission's completed
work and trigger a full re-plan loop. Failure should be CONTAINED to the step, not amplified to the whole mission.

Maqsad: a step failure is ISOLATED — the engine repairs/retries the FAILED STEP, and if it still can't, it FAILS that
step (or its sub-mission) with a receipt while PRESERVING every completed step; it does NOT discard completed work and
re-plan the entire mission. A mission that completed 5 of 8 steps then hit a transient must resume/repair from step 6,
not restart from step 1.

REQUIRED BEHAVIOR (verify the current path in orchestrate.mjs before changing):
1. CHECKPOINT completed steps: the artifacts + receipts of steps that already passed PERSIST and are not re-done on a
   later-step failure. (Today a re-plan re-runs everything — wasteful + non-convergent.)
2. On a step failure after its repair rounds are exhausted: do NOT full-re-plan by default. Options to implement (pick
   the cleanest, witnessed): (a) retry the SAME step with a fresh dispatch (a transient empty emission deserves a plain
   retry, distinct from a content repair); (b) if a genuine plan flaw, re-plan ONLY the remaining steps from the failed
   point, feeding the completed-step receipts as fixed context; (c) only full-re-plan if the whole approach is invalid.
3. A TRANSIENT (empty emission / network) must be distinguished from a real defect (witness REJECT on bad content):
   transient -> retry the step; real defect -> repair, then fail-the-step-with-receipt. Never silently loop.
4. Bound it: a step gets K retries then the mission FAILS at that step with a receipt naming the step + reason — no
   infinite re-plan loop, no discarded work.
5. STATE CLEANUP BEFORE RETRY (laguna witness 2026-06-16, finding #1 — a real gap in the first draft): a failed step may
   have left CORRUPTED PARTIAL STATE (a half-written file, a partial commit). Retrying the step is UNSAFE until that
   partial state is rolled back to the step's pre-attempt checkpoint. So: before re-attempting a step, RESTORE the target
   to its last-good checkpoint (the integrity-guard/rollback machinery in orchestrate already snapshots — use it). Never
   retry onto dirty state.
6. SUB-MISSION FAILURE OWNERSHIP (laguna finding #4 — the A/B boundary): this step-isolation policy is what an
   auto-split SUB-MISSION (M-ENGINE.HAJJ-AUTOSPLIT.1) inherits — define it ONCE here and have sub-missions inherit it, so
   "who owns retry/failure within a split child" is unambiguous: each sub-mission is a normal mission and gets THIS
   step-isolation behavior; a sub-mission that fails-at-a-step fails just itself (its tartib successors hold), it does not
   re-plan the parent. Build B and A to share this one failure-boundary definition.
