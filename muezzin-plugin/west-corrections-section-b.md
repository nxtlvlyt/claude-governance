# West Corrections — Section B

## Vegreville Pysanka Date Correction

**Claim as written**: The Vegreville Pysanka was unveiled in 1976.

**Corrected NOTES**: The Vegreville Pysanka was unveiled on July 2, 1975, marking the official opening of the landmark Ukrainian Easter egg sculpture in Vegreville, Alberta — the world's largest pysanka, confirmed by the town's official website. [Source: vegreville.com/worlds-largest-pysanka](https://vegreville.com/worlds-largest-pysanka)
