Maqsad: give the local conductor seat the ability to REACH. Today `conduct-beat-local.mjs`
digests the board into a summary, sends ONE prompt, and parses ONE allowlisted verb
(report / hold / restart / record). The seat is handed a pre-chewed state and asked for a
verdict. It has no probe, no second turn, and therefore no way to find anything out. Add a
read-only `probe` verb and the one extra turn that makes it a loop.

WHY THIS IS THE RIGHT LEVER, receipted 2026-08-02 — do NOT re-solve this by retraining:

  * `CLAUDE.md` D2 teaches the behaviour verbatim: "Your first move on uncertainty is
    action, not consultation."
  * `train-v33.jsonl` CANNOT teach it: 323/360 targets (89%) name a probe, but only
    38/360 report what a probe RETURNED, and **0 of 360 rows have more than one turn** —
    every row is exactly [system, user, assistant]. There is no shape in that dataset in
    which reach -> tool answers -> ship can be written down. This is a DATA-SHAPE limit,
    not a rank/epoch/lr problem, and retrying it as one is the recorded failure mode.
  * It is not a capability gap either. In `phase3/fullloop-v1.py` the tuned 27B, GIVEN the
    three turns by the harness, handled the returned tool output correctly 7/8 — including
    the EMPTY and ERROR cases, where it named the gap and invented nothing.
  * And nemotron-3-ultra (550B), which never saw this corpus, also shipped after reaching
    ZERO times. The behaviour is rare in general. The harness has to supply the loop.

niyyah: the loop is added WITHOUT widening what the seat may do. The probe verb is READ-ONLY
and allowlisted by name; it can never mutate the board, the repo, or a mission. The existing
gateAction ruling, the per-turn rijal JSONL record, and the think-false flag are reused as
they stand — this mission adds a verb and a turn, not a new execution path. A seat that can
read more must not thereby be able to write more.
